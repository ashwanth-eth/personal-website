Seenly

A photo-sharing app for weddings. We used it at 6 weddings so guests could share their photos.

Guests uploaded photos as the day went on, and face matching let each person find every photo they were in. The couple got all of it in one place, without hashtags or manual uploads.

It's retired now, since all of my friends are married.
