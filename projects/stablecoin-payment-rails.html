Stablecoin Payment Rails

Overview

A high-performance payment infrastructure built on stablecoin technology, enabling instant cross-border transactions with minimal fees. The system is designed for scalability and compliance with modern regulatory frameworks.

The Challenge

Traditional cross-border payments are slow, expensive, and opaque. Businesses and individuals need a way to move value across borders instantly, with full transparency and at a fraction of the cost of legacy systems.

The Solution

We built a payment infrastructure that leverages stablecoins to provide instant settlement, transparent pricing, and programmable money features. The system includes:

  • Real-time settlement with sub-second transaction finality
  • Multi-currency support with automatic conversion
  • Compliance-first architecture with built-in KYC/AML checks
  • API-first design for easy integration
  • Smart contract-based escrow and conditional payments

Technical Stack

Built with Ethereum Layer 2 for scalability, utilizing USDC and other regulated stablecoins. The backend is powered by a microservices architecture with event-driven processing for high throughput.

Impact

The platform now processes over $10M in monthly transaction volume, serving businesses across 25 countries. Average transaction costs reduced by 85% compared to traditional wire transfers, with settlement times reduced from 3-5 days to under 30 seconds.
